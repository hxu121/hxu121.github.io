Welcome! I am Hao Xu, currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow (in semiconductor nanosensors) in the [Nano-Optoelectronics Lab](https://nano-oelab.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/Home/Jsxx/jsxx_1?lang=en&id=810) at Tsinghua University. Prior to here, I received my PhD (in nanoelectronics) at the Institute for Electronics, Microelectronics and Nanotechnology ([IEMN](https://www.iemn.fr/en/)), CNRS/Univ. Lille, France.

## Research

I am interested in realizing micro and nanosystems & sensors in the optoelectronics. Engineering a nanoelectronic sensor in the tiny world is notoriously tough, and it most of time does not work well. Therefore, my interest for a novel device design lies in device modeling and simulation for predicting the potential performance; as following, device fabrication and measurements could be done. My expertise skills include
1. advanced manufacturing (micro and nanofabrication in cleanroom);
2. nanoelectromechanical device modeling (mathematical modeling, multiphysics simulation);
3. hands-on experiments (microwave, electrical, optical readout).

my research goal aims at engineering-guided nanosensors making humans live healthier and safer. For example, nanoelectronic NEMS and **semiconductor nanolasers** are involved in quantum circuits sensors, CMOS-compatible device technology and biomedical imaging.

<img src="https://hxu121.github.io/images/laserarray_102026.jpg?raw=true" alt="Photo" style="width: 500px;"/><br/> _Nanolaser array_

In particular, [AFM-tip/membrane](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.nanolett.4c01401) electronic interface and principles in the nanoscale have been studied during my PhD study. Then future plan, my research further explores the neuroelectronic device (in NeuronTech), including device engineering design, proof-of-concept principles and neural signal acquisition & processing. These problems-solving techniques will benefit for the disabilities (e.g., paralyzed people).
